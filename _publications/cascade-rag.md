Long-document question answering requires selecting and ordering evidence from many
candidate passages. Cascade Retrieval decomposes this into a cheap span index and a
progressive reranking stage that spends budget only on ambiguous regions. The released
implementation includes the full pipeline, the experiment scripts used for the ACL
suite and the archived result tables.
